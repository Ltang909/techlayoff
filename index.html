Laid off from tech recently? Come talk to people in the same spot.

The Tech Layoff Club pairs you with another tech worker who was recently laid off for a curated 1:1 coffee chat. Voice, video, or text, your pick. No agenda and no pitch. Just a real conversation with someone who understands the situation, because they are in it too. A full calendar used to come with the job. The good conversations do not have to stop with it.

About the founder

I was recently laid off from a Series B tech startup. Ten years in marketing, and I never expected to be here. The company had been struggling, losing deals to AI, restructuring more than once.

Then came the rejections, the side business attempts, and a quieter problem: I missed talking to people who get it. And I was too shy to cold-message anyone on LinkedIn about it.

So I am building the thing I went looking for. A place where people in the same transition can talk to each other, human to human. Some chats will be about job leads. Some will just be two people comparing notes and laughing about the absurd parts. Both count.

What this is not

Not a job board. Job leads may come up in conversation, and that is fine, but nobody is here to pitch you.

Not a networking event. No name tags, no elevator pitches, no crowd to work.

Not a giant group chat. Two people, one conversation. Small groups may come later, only if members ask for them.

Anonymous by default

Use a nickname if you want. We ask for your LinkedIn to verify your background. There is no public profile and nothing gets posted anywhere. Your match only sees what you choose to share with them.
