Recently laid off? You don't have to sit in it alone.

Tech Layoff Club pairs laid-off tech workers for one-to-one coffee chats. Every match is made by a person, not an algorithm. You can stay anonymous. There is no job board, no feed, and nothing to perform for.

How it works

  1. Join the waitlist. Email, plus a few short questions so the match is a good one.
  2. We match you by hand with someone in a similar spot.
  3. You talk. Voice, video, or text. Whatever feels easiest.

What this is not

Not a job board. Job leads may come up in conversation, and that is fine, but nobody is here to pitch you.

Not a networking event. No name tags, no elevator pitches, no crowd to work.

Not a giant group chat. Two people, one conversation. Small groups may come later, only if members ask for them.
